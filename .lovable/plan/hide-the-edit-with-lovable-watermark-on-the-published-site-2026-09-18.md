# Hide the "Edit with Lovable" watermark on the published site

## Why it appears
The "Edit with Lovable" badge is switched ON by default for this project. Publishing alone does not remove it — it is a separate project setting.

## What I will do
1. Switch the badge setting to hidden for this project.
2. Re-publish is not needed for the setting itself, but a fresh publish ensures it takes effect on the live site.

## Important note
Hiding the badge requires a Pro plan or higher. If the workspace is on a Free plan, the badge cannot be removed and the setting will be refused — in that case the options are upgrading the plan or keeping the badge.
